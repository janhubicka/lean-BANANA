# Classical dependencies of the BANANA circulation draft

This audit covers the classical results used in the circulation proofs at
manuscript commit [7ef8d62](https://github.com/janhubicka/BANANA/commit/7ef8d62c63f5a5348b625e386d26d5a1f1439e4e).
The local checks used Lean 4.35.0-rc3 and mathlib
[5bd58ac](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/tree/5bd58ac291422a21f412ae354c91e7d172255a2c).

## Model theory and Ramsey theory

| Result needed | Coverage and next use |
| --- | --- |
| Fraïssé existence and uniqueness for the finite classes | Mathlib's Fraïssé API provides age, hereditary property, joint embedding, ordinary amalgamation, back-and-forth and uniqueness. Its countable age-realisation result does not itself give homogeneity. Cameron Freer's existence theorem was compiled from four unmodified files on BANANA's mathlib pin. Its axiom report lists only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. It takes an IsFraisse class in a language with countably many function symbols and returns a countable Fraïssé limit. The first-order Folkman product encoding uses coordinate projections, ring operations, zero and the pairing. Lean proves that embeddings between encoded structures are exactly the native non-unital pairing-preserving maps. The next step is to transfer the finite class certificate and instantiate the existence theorem. [Folkman embedding correspondence](../BANANA/NonPrecompact/FolkmanFirstOrder.lean) [External Fraïssé existence theorem](https://github.com/cameronfreer/computable-model-theory/blob/cafefc908d8537008f26951b7b197bff7ad0001e/ComputableModelTheory/ModelTheory/FraisseExistence.lean#L282) |
| Ryll–Nardzewski | The class proofs use that finitely many automorphism orbits at each tuple length imply omega-categoricity. The equivalence also enters the group and expansion formulations of Ivanov's question. No matching full Lean theorem was found in the audited sources. The shortest target for the class proofs is the forward direction for the explicit homogeneous locally finite models. Use finite atomic diagrams to isolate the tuple types, transfer the extension sentences, then apply the existing first-order back-and-forth. Freer's orbit-isolation and countable-atomic model lemmas also compiled unchanged on the current pin. They give a one-point extension step for matching formula types, which supports this route. [Orbit isolation](https://github.com/cameronfreer/computable-model-theory/blob/cafefc908d8537008f26951b7b197bff7ad0001e/ComputableModelTheory/ModelTheory/OrbitIsolation.lean) |
| Binary Graham–Leeb–Rothschild theorem | The one-sided degree-one argument needs monochromatic subspaces of fixed dimension inside a larger subspace over the two-element field. The binary version and its applications to both sides already follow from the formal successor-tree theorem. [Binary GLR and one-sided degrees](../BANANA/NonPrecompact/LinearBinaryRepresentability.lean) |
| Witt extension for the alternating form over the two-element field | Lean proves the extension from any finite-dimensional subspace, including a degenerate one, to a global isometric automorphism of a countable infinite-dimensional nondegenerate alternating space over any field. The proof uses mathlib's duality for prescribed pairings and its first-order back-and-forth theorem. Lean also constructs the concrete countable orthogonal sum of hyperbolic planes, identifies its finite age and instantiates the extension theorem there. [Countable Witt extension](../BANANA/NonPrecompact/WittCountable.lean) · [Concrete symplectic model](../BANANA/NonPrecompact/SymplecticCountableModel.lean) |
| Hrushovski–Krupiński–Pillay Proposition 2.17 and Krupiński–Lee–Moconja Theorem 3.16(ii) | To derive DEERP, use the forward implications from omega-categoricity and canonical amalgamation over the empty-generated base to first-order extreme amenability, and from first-order extreme amenability to DEERP. No matching Lean endpoints were found. BANANA has a native canonical zero-base amalgamation theorem. Keep the model-theoretic implications explicit as hypotheses of a partial validation. [Canonical zero-base amalgamation](../BANANA/NonPrecompact/BananaDirectSumCanonical.lean) · [HKP](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00029-026-01125-1) · [KLM](https://doi.org/10.1090/tran/8594) |

## Topological dynamics and measures

| Result needed | Coverage and next use |
| --- | --- |
| Kechris–Rosendal Theorem 6.2 | The direction needed is that JEP and WAP for finite systems of partial automorphisms yield a comeagre diagonal conjugacy orbit on each finite power of the automorphism group. The finite systems are checked in Lean. The Baire and topological group machinery exists, with no matching theorem found in the audited libraries. Keep the conclusion conditional on the criterion. [Finite-system verification](../BANANA/NonPrecompact/BananaFiniteSystemsJepWap.lean) |
| Ben Yaacov–Melleray–Tsankov and Zucker | Lemma EA-degree uses the proof of BMT Corollary 3.3 for metrisability from a closed co-precompact extremely amenable subgroup of a Polish group, then Zucker Theorem 8.7 for finite embedding degrees when the universal minimal flow is metrisable. Finite embedding degrees bound copy degrees. The nonmetrisability proof also uses BMT Theorem 1.1 in the reverse direction. No matching formalisation was found. A direct proof of the degree bound can use the fixed point in the orbit closure of a colouring, finitely many subgroup-orbits on copies, and a finite-support compactness argument. The last step is now available locally. It still needs the group action and finite ambient structure wrappers. For the nonmetrisability conclusion alone, the forward implication in Zucker 8.7 and the infinite copy degree also suffice by contraposition. [Finite-support compactness](../BANANA/NonPrecompact/FiniteColouringCompactness.lean) · [BMT](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.01068) · [Zucker](https://math.cmu.edu/~andrewz/GPP.pdf) |
| Covering criterion for co-precompact subgroups | Melleray–Nguyen Van Thé–Tsankov gives the finite UFH covering formulation for the right uniformity. The new double-coset lemma proves the algebraic equivalence of a finite cover at a point stabiliser and finitely many subgroup-orbits. The topology still needs neighbourhood bases of finite tuple stabilisers. Ivanov's equivalent formulations also need the closure and canonical orbit expansion facts. [Double-coset bridge](../BANANA/NonPrecompact/OrbitCover.lean) |
| Universal minimal flows and Polish automorphism groups | The dynamical argument also uses the classical existence and uniqueness of the universal minimal flow, and closedness of the countable automorphism group in the pointwise permutation topology. The former supplies the background to the BMT and Zucker statements. Construction of these interfaces in the manuscript's topology remains external. |
| Amenability of dense unions of finite groups | Needed for the BANANA automorphism group and the groups preserving positive masses. The algebraic approximation is already checked for BANANA. Mathlib gives compactness of probability measures on compact Hausdorff Borel spaces, weak-continuous pushforward and real Riesz–Markov–Kakutani. A local proof can use finite orbit averages, impose closed equations on integrals of continuous functions, then regularise and use uniqueness to obtain an invariant regular probability measure. This avoids assuming that weak convergence separates arbitrary nonregular measures. No matching end-to-end group theorem was found. [Prokhorov compactness](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/5bd58ac291422a21f412ae354c91e7d172255a2c/Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Measure/Prokhorov.lean) |
| Amenability passes to open subgroups | Used for the Folkman stabiliser. The coinduction proof cited in the article is stated for extreme amenability. For amenability it uses the same compact coinduced space and pushes an invariant probability forward by evaluation at the identity. This amenability variant still needs a Lean proof. |
| Stone representation and extension of a clopen charge | Used for nonamenability and for the positive-mass subgroups. Mathlib identifies idempotents with clopens in a prime spectrum. On a Boolean ring, every element is idempotent. The new Boolean spectrum module gives a compact Hausdorff space with all clopens represented by Boolean elements, and verifies equivariance under Boolean isomorphisms. It proves joint continuity from open inverse-evaluation fibres. The actual pointwise automorphism action still needs to be instantiated. Mathlib also has real Riesz–Markov–Kakutani, with the passage from the clopen charge to the positive functional still to implement. For the headline positive-mass conclusions, amenability can instead supply an invariant measure whose clopen values are forced by the finite transitive subgroups. For the nonamenability contradiction it is enough to use the closed space of Boolean characters and integrate the coordinate indicators. [Boolean spectrum](../BANANA/NonPrecompact/BooleanSpectrum.lean) · [Prime spectrum](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/5bd58ac291422a21f412ae354c91e7d172255a2c/Mathlib/RingTheory/Spectrum/Prime/Topology.lean) · [Riesz representation](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/5bd58ac291422a21f412ae354c91e7d172255a2c/Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Integral/RieszMarkovKakutani/Real.lean) |
| Compactness and Baire category for two-adic charges | Mathlib has compact p-adic integers, reductions to prime-power residue rings, the inverse-limit lift, and the Baire property of compact Hausdorff spaces. The new Baire lemma proves the last meagreness and non-comeagreness step from continuous evaluation and empty-interior fibres in a countable range. The closed charge space, perturbation of the fibres, continuity of the action and minimality still need their project-specific proofs. [Baire bridge](../BANANA/NonPrecompact/PrebananaBaireOrbit.lean) |

An external Stone duality repository also has a categorical equivalence
between profinite spaces and the opposite category of Boolean algebras.
It uses Lean 4.8.0-rc1. The direct Boolean spectrum module uses the prime-spectrum API of the current mathlib pin.
[External Stone duality](https://github.com/samvang/StoneDualityInLean/blob/4beff7bdc4647c0fe06fda9ad2b8d740bdfc16e4/StoneDuality/Boolean.lean#L821)

For partial validation, state missing results as hypotheses of the
relevant theorem. This keeps them visible in the theorem statement and
allows the checked implications to pass the ordinary axiom audit.

places. The published-version numbering is Question 7.3 for the Ivanov
question and Definition 6.4, Lemma 6.6 and Theorem 6.7 for the enriched
classes. This is a citation issue in the introduction. [Published EHN](https://doi.org/10.1112/plms.12238)
