# BANANA Track C — adversarial proof note (internal, 1 November 2026)

**Scope.** This note is not manuscript prose and is not a Lean
verification certificate. The circulation part remains frozen. The
related proposed Lean code is in draft PR #308 stacked on draft #306.

## Exact finite statement

Let `A` be a finite two-sorted Folkman atom model and let `x_i`,
`y_j` be labelled tuples of elements of the two *non-unital* Boolean
rings. For each ambient atom `a`, let `sig_x(a)` record membership in
the tuple coordinates. Let `P_x` be the set of realised nonzero
signatures, and `F_p` the fibre with signature `p`. Define `P_y`
and `G_q` on the right sort. The complete tuple profile is

`(P_x, P_y, M)`, where `M(p,q) = beta(F_p,G_q)` in `F_2`, with
rows/columns for the all-zero signatures forced to zero.

**Claim.** Equal complete profiles give a generator-preserving,
pairing-preserving isomorphism of the two finitely generated **non-unital**
two-sort structures. This does not imply that the full ambient finite
models are isomorphic, nor does it by itself yield an automorphism of
the countable limit.

## First independent mathematical argument: support decomposition

1. Any ring term in `x_i`, made using `0`, symmetric difference and
   intersection, has membership constant on each `F_p`. It vanishes
   on all atoms with zero signature. Thus every generated element is a
   union of nonzero signature fibres.
2. Conversely, for each realised nonzero `p`, choose `i` with
   `p(i)=1`. Then
   `F_p = (intersection over j with p(j)=1 of x_j) minus
   (union over j with p(j)=0 of x_j)`.
   The positive intersection is nonempty as a family; therefore it is
   formed using generators without the ambient unit. Relative
   difference is `a minus b = a + a*b`, and union is
   `a union b = a + b + a*b` in a Boolean ring. Hence `F_p` is in the
   ring generated by the tuple. This construction also covers a
   single positive coordinate and empty negative-coordinate family.
3. The generated non-unital ring is therefore canonically the Boolean
   ring of subsets of `P_x`: the subset `S` codes the disjoint union
   of `F_p` for `p in S`. Addition is symmetric difference,
   multiplication is intersection and zero is the empty subset. The
   labelled coordinate `x_i` corresponds to
   `{p in P_x : p(i)=1}`. The same construction applies to `P_y`.
4. Bilinearity gives
   `beta(union_{p in S} F_p, union_{q in T} G_q) =
   sum_{p in S,q in T} M(p,q)` in `F_2`. Thus equal complete
   profiles define the two-sort isomorphism on all generated elements,
   including the pairing, not only their tuple coordinates.

This argument makes no hypothesis that the ambient rings are
nontrivial or their atom sets nonempty.

## Second independent approach: term-equality kernels

Reuse `PrebananaBooleanTerm` but restrict to terms `t` satisfying
`t.evalPattern(0)=false`. Equality of their evaluations is equivalent
to equality of their Boolean truth functions on the realised *nonzero*
patterns. Equal profiles therefore yield identical term-equality
kernels, and the same-term evaluation map gives a canonical bijection
between generated term-value ranges. This is the route implemented by
the unverified proposed Lean statements in
`FolkmanTupleTermEquations.lean` and
`FolkmanTupleTermPairIso.lean`.

**Remaining bridge:** identify this restricted Boolean-term range with
the native subring generated by the tuple, proving the closure and
term-normalisation statements in Lean, and explicitly package its ring
operations. Be especially careful to use a **non-unital** subring
construction. An ordinary unital `Subring.closure` is not appropriate.

## Adversarial boundary tests

- **Ambient unit trap:** in the two-element ambient Boolean ring, an
  empty tuple (or a single zero generator) generates only `{0}` in
  the non-unital language. A unital closure would add `1` and give an
  incorrect classification. The all-zero signature may occur on
  nonempty ambient atom sets, and must be ignored.
- **Pairing matrix is essential:** take one left and one right atom,
  both named by their single tuple coordinate. Incidence matrices
  `[0]` and `[1]` give identical nonzero signature sets but different
  two-sort generated structures.
- **Unused atoms matter only to the ambient model:** changing the
  number of zero-signature atoms or their incidence with named atoms
  does not alter the generated non-unital structure.
- **Degeneracy:** one or both atom sorts can have zero atoms; tuple
  lengths can be zero independently; `P_x` or `P_y` can be empty.
  The sum formula still gives zero pairing where appropriate.
- **Formalisation fidelity:** profile invariance under partial owners
  requires nonempty source fibres, supplied by the existing
  `FolkmanAtomEmbedding` fields. The finite profile-completeness
  argument itself does **not** assume an embedding.
- **Countable step:** obtaining automorphism-orbit classification
  requires the homogeneous countable Folkman limit and extension of
  isomorphisms of finite generated structures. This is a dependency on
  Track B, not supplied by the finite tuple-profile argument.
